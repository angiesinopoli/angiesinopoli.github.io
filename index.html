About me

resourceful /rəˈsôrsf(ə)l/

Being resourceful means working through a problem with whatever tools and information are already available, and staying level-headed when a situation is unfamiliar or unclear. It is the trait I rely on most.

I'm Angie, a Computer & Information Science student at The Ohio State University. My degree is an individualized specialization, which is how I'm keeping my options open while I work out where I fit best in tech. I grew up near Cleveland and started coding in high school, but it was my harder college courses that showed me how much I enjoy being challenged by it. School came easily to me for a long time, so the first problems that took real effort to solve were also the most satisfying.

I'm practical, independent, and resourceful, and I don't fit the usual picture of someone in tech. I'm outgoing and people-oriented, and being able to explain technical things clearly, build good working relationships, and put people at ease is one of my strongest skills.

Most of my hands-on programming experience is in Java, and I have experience with Python, C, JavaScript, and HTML/CSS through coursework and projects. Alongside school I've been working in enterprise IT at Westfield Insurance, which has shown me how technology operates inside a large company. I'm still deciding where I want to focus, whether that's development, systems, data, or a mix, but I want to keep working on real problems and building things I'm proud of. Outside of class and work, I'm usually at the gym, out with friends, or at home with my dogs, Thor and Sparkle.
