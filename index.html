about me

resourceful /rəˈsôrsf(ə)l/

being resourceful means having the cleverness and initiative to work through problems and overcome difficulties, often by making good use of whatever tools or information are already at hand. it is a trait built on adaptability, creative thinking, and staying steady when a situation is new, or a little messy.

hi! i'm Angie, a BS-CIS student at The Ohio State University with an individualized specialization, which is basically my way of keeping my options open while i figure out exactly where i fit best in tech. i grew up near Cleveland and first got into coding in high school, but it was not until my harder college classes that i realized how much i actually liked being challenged by it. school had always come pretty easily to me, so having to really sit with a problem until it clicked made finally solving it that much more satisfying.

i'm practical, independent, and resourceful, but i do not always fit the mold people expect in tech. i'm outgoing and people oriented, and i think my ability to communicate clearly, build relationships, and make people feel comfortable is one of my biggest strengths, especially in a field where that combination isn't always the default.

most of my hands-on experience is in Java and HTML/CSS, and i'm picking up Python and JavaScript along the way, most recently through an internship supporting enterprise technology at Westfield Insurance. i'm still deciding exactly where i want to land, whether that's development, systems, data, or something that mixes a little of everything, but i know i want to keep working through real problems, building things i'm proud of, and proving i belong in the room. outside of school and work, i'm usually at the gym, trying a new coffee shop, spending time with friends, or at home with my dogs, Thor and Sparkle.

my work

  • LSAT Liaison

    an AI tutoring tool that helps LSAT students actually understand their mistakes instead of just seeing the right answer. built around structured prompt design, reasoning-pattern analysis, and an adaptive, Socratic teaching style that asks guiding questions instead of lecturing.

  • Dupe Finder

    a full-stack app that helps you find ingredient-based dupes for makeup and skincare, so you're not paying more for the same formula. built with Java Spring Boot on the backend, React on the front end, and the OpenBeautyFacts API for ingredient data, my first project taken all the way from database to a UI people would actually want to use.
