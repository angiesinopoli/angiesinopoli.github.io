About me

resourceful /rəˈsôrsf(ə)l/

Being resourceful means working through a problem with whatever tools and information I already have, and keeping a level head when a situation is new or unclear. It's the trait I lean on most.

I'm Angie, a Computer & Information Science student at The Ohio State University, graduating in May 2028. I grew up near Cleveland and started coding in high school. My individualized specialization pairs computer science with business coursework, because the problems I find most interesting usually involve people and organizations as much as code.

I've worked in enterprise IT support, IT financial planning, and help desk and classroom technology support, and I build software on my own time. Across all of it, I like the same things: figuring out how a system works, finding a fix that holds up, and explaining it clearly enough that the next person doesn't have to start over. I'm outgoing and people-oriented, so the conversations around technical work matter to me as much as the work itself.

That mix is what draws me to software development, solutions engineering, and technology consulting. Outside of class and work, I'm usually at the gym, out with friends, or home with my dogs, Thor and Sparkle.

My work

  • LSAT Liaison

    Custom GPT · Personal project

    A custom GPT I designed for structured LSAT tutoring. I wrote the instructions that shape how it teaches: breaking questions down by reasoning type, explaining why wrong answers are wrong, and asking guiding questions instead of handing over the answer, so each session works like an interactive lesson.

    Prompt engineering · Instructional design · Structured reasoning

  • Dupe Finder

    Full-stack web app · Personal project

    A beauty product comparison app that finds possible alternatives to a cosmetic product based on what's in the formula. I built it end to end: a Spring Boot backend that pulls ingredient data from the OpenBeautyFacts API, matching logic that scores products by Jaccard similarity, and a React front end for searching and comparing results.

    Java · Spring Boot · React · REST APIs

  • Offboarding Follow-Up Automation

    Case study · Westfield Insurance

    A PowerShell workflow that closes gaps in employee offboarding. It pulls separation data with SQL, checks whether managers completed the required offboarding tickets, generates a monthly spreadsheet of completed and missing tickets, and drafts reminder emails for anything outstanding. Collaborated on development with another intern and a mentor, splitting the workflow into separate methods. The finished automation was implemented.

    PowerShell · SQL · Automation

  • IT Troubleshooting Decision Tool

    Guided support tool · In progress

    A guided troubleshooting tool based on my enterprise IT support experience. It walks users through common tech problems with plain-language questions and clear next steps, and points them to a real person only when it's needed.
